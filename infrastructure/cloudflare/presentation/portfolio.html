Insurance operations with accountable AI

An independent reference implementation by Kimani Mutua. A working Java and Python application explores partner billing and hospital cash claims using invented customers, documents and policy terms.

The problem

Partner files arrive with different column names. Claims arrive with partial documents and corrected dates. A payment timeout may leave an uncertain outcome. Each case needs explicit authority, durable history and a safe recovery path.

The implementation separates model suggestions from business decisions. Humans approve mappings, evidence and simulated invoices. Java validates records, calculates decimal totals and enforces transitions.

Implemented workflows

Partner data to invoice preparation

Upload a small synthetic CSV, inspect the proposed column mapping, review exceptions, explicitly exclude invalid rows and approve valid records. The system prepares one scoped partner/month invoice result. Retries preserve its identity.

Claims and document completeness

Versioned requirements apply from the incident date. Uploading a document does not verify it. Wrong customer associations, conflicting documents and stale facts block readiness. Authorized reviewers record verification or an approver records a separate waiver.

Claim review and recovery

Customer confirmation precedes review and deterministic benefit reservation. An unknown simulated payout blocks a blind retry. A separate mock receipt supports reconciliation and preserves the original attempt.

Channel rehearsals

Notification previews enforce consent, identity, quiet hours and throttling. Typed voice rehearsals answer grounded synthetic questions and hand off uncertain requests. They send no messages and process no audio.

Architecture and tradeoffs

Authority
Spring Boot and PostgreSQL own state, scoped access, encrypted evidence, audit events and the transactional outbox.
AI boundary
Deep Agents proposes mappings from schema metadata through two constrained tools. Mock inference is the demo default. Bedrock is opt-in and bounded by region, model, time and request budget settings.
Experience
React offers separate claims and billing workspaces. Keycloak supplies demonstration roles and tokens.
Hosting
Cloudflare serves this case study and presentation independently of the Codespace that runs the application. The demo can sleep. ECS Fargate and RDS are selected future targets.
