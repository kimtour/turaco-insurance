Demo sequence

  1. Create and confirm an intake. Use the sample hospital stay, record confirmation and assisted identity/payee verification.
  2. Record the decision. Submit, recommend approval and reserve the benefit. Three covered nights produce a simulated KES 4,500 under the invented policy.
  3. Show an uncertain payout. Create an UNKNOWN outcome. Record a separate mock receipt and reconcile. Show that the original attempt was preserved.
  4. Inspect a correction. Request more information, change a field, then confirm and resubmit. Conflicting evidence needs a reviewed matching replacement.
